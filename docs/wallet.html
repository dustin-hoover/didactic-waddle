⚠️
This moves real funds. NEAR Intents fills cross-chain swaps by having you send the origin token to a one-time deposit address; solvers deliver the output to your wallet on the destination chain. You sign the deposit transfer in your own wallet — no key ever lives here. Honest caveat: during the fill (~30s) the 1Click service temporarily custodies the funds, so keep amounts canary-sized.
