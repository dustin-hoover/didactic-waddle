# Procurement / Inventory Coordinator

**Reports to:** COO/Controller · **Type:** Full-time · **Location:** NW Arkansas

## Summary
Make sure the right materials are on the right boat at the right time, at the right
price, with clean records for accounting and grant compliance. Run the BOM-driven
ordering process (docs 10–11).

## Responsibilities
- Turn released work orders into material pulls and POs via the BOM kits.
- Manage inventory across warehouse, zone caches, and boat kits; set/tune reorder
  points and safety stock on outage-critical spares.
- Manage vendors (primary + alternate), get quotes (3-quote rule), negotiate blanket
  POs for high-run items, and manage lead times.
- Flag and document **grant-funded** purchases and **BABA/domestic-content** compliance
  before ordering.
- Receive/QC, track serials, handle RMA/warranty; ensure CapEx is captured per WO.

## Requirements
- Purchasing/inventory experience; organized, detail-oriented, systems-minded.
- Comfort with spreadsheets/CSV and inventory software; vendor negotiation.

## Nice to have
- Telecom/construction materials experience; grant-compliance/BABA familiarity.

## Comp
Competitive base.
