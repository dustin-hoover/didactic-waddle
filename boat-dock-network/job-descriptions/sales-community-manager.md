# Sales / Community Manager

**Reports to:** CEO · **Type:** Full-time · **Location:** Beaver Lake, NW Arkansas

## Summary
Turn the lake community into customers and host nodes. Drive zone-by-zone pre-sales,
recruit host-node sites, and build BDN's local brand as "the lake's own internet
company."

## Responsibilities
- Run **pre-sales** per zone (deposits/sign-ups) to gate builds by demand.
- Recruit **host nodes** (best high points, docks, marinas) — coverage + anchor
  customers in one.
- Land marina/resort/business accounts as lighthouse deployments.
- Work lake associations, HOAs, chambers, and local events; build referral programs.
- Feed demand data + site leads into GIS/DockOS; support grant community-support
  letters.

## Requirements
- Local sales/community-engagement skill; self-directed; great with people.
- Comfort explaining internet options plainly; CRM discipline.

## Nice to have
- NW Arkansas/Beaver Lake network; telecom/ISP sales; boating familiarity.

## Comp
Base + commission/pre-sales incentives.
