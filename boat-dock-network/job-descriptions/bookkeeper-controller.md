# Bookkeeper / Controller

**Reports to:** CEO · **Type:** Contract → full-time · **Location:** Remote/NW Arkansas

## Summary
Keep the books clean and audit-ready — with the segregated, well-documented accounting
that grants and telecom lenders require. Capture CapEx accurately by work order and
support the funding stack (doc 09).

## Responsibilities
- Own the telecom-flavored chart of accounts; monthly close; financial reporting.
- **Capitalize construction** correctly (labor + materials → assets); maintain
  depreciation schedules by asset class.
- Run **grant compliance accounting**: segregated funds, documented match, draw
  packages, audit trails.
- Manage AP/AR, payroll coordination, payments (Stripe reconciliation), and taxes
  (sales/use, FCC fees, state/county).
- Support the pro forma with actuals; partner with procurement on PO→asset capture.

## Requirements
- Bookkeeping/controller experience; GAAP; strong systems/spreadsheet skills.
- Grant or government-funding compliance experience (or fast learner).

## Nice to have
- Telecom/utility/construction accounting; CPA; RUS/BEAD reporting familiarity.

## Comp
Competitive; contract acceptable early, scaling with the company.
