# Marine Field Operations Lead

**Reports to:** CEO/COO · **Type:** Full-time · **Location:** Beaver Lake, NW Arkansas

## Summary
Run boat-native field operations — construction, installs, and repairs delivered by
water. Own crews, fleet, safety, and schedule so nodes get built and customers get
connected efficiently and safely on a 449-mile shoreline.

## Responsibilities
- Lead field crews (build + service); plan daily boat dispatch and zone caching.
- Own the **fleet** (work pontoons, fast boats, trailers) and its maintenance.
- Own **safety**: USCG compliance, weather go/no-go, RF/climbing/electrical safety,
  and **zero-spill** drinking-water protection near the reservoir.
- Execute node builds (T1–T3), drops, and CPE installs to standard; enforce as-built.
- Hit install-cycle-time, MTTR, and quality KPIs; run weekly ops reviews.
- Coordinate with NOC (dispatch), GIS (as-builts), and procurement (materials/kits).

## Requirements
- Field construction/telecom leadership; boat operation and lake safety.
- Fiber and/or wireless install experience; crew management; scheduling.
- Practical problem-solver comfortable in remote, on-water conditions.

## Nice to have
- USCG captain's license; FOA fiber cert; tower/climbing cert; WISP/FTTH build history.

## Comp
Competitive base + on-call/marine premium + safety-tied bonus.
