# Regulatory & Permitting Lead

**Reports to:** CEO · **Type:** Full-time / part-time / contract · **Location:** NW Arkansas

## Summary
Clear the path for construction. Own the permitting function — especially the USACE
shoreline/lakebed process that makes or breaks the schedule — plus FCC, pole
attachments, ARDOT, and county approvals (doc 12).

## Responsibilities
- Lead the **USACE Little Rock District** relationship: shoreline use permits, real-
  estate outgrants/easements, Section 10/404 for any in-water work.
- Negotiate **pole-attachment agreements** (Ozarks Electric, Carroll Electric, SWEPCO)
  and manage make-ready.
- Handle **FCC** matters: ASN/499 setup support, licensed-microwave coordination,
  CBRS/AFC, FAA/ASR where applicable.
- Manage **ARDOT** and **county ROW/zoning** permits and **AR 811** processes.
- Maintain the permit-rules base feeding the DockOS compliance advisor; keep per-WO
  permit checklists accurate.

## Requirements
- Telecom/utility permitting or land-use/right-of-way experience.
- Ability to work with federal (USACE/FCC), state, and county agencies.
- Detail-oriented process management; clear documentation.

## Nice to have
- USACE reservoir/shoreline experience; pole-attachment negotiation; NEPA/Section 106
  familiarity; Arkansas agency relationships.

## Comp
Competitive; contract acceptable early.
