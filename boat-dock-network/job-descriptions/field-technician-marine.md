# Fiber/Wireless Field Technician (Marine)

**Reports to:** Marine Field Operations Lead · **Type:** Full-time · **Location:** Beaver Lake, NW Arkansas

## Summary
The specialized boat tech at the heart of the company: you travel the lake by boat to
build nodes, install fiber and wireless service, and repair the network — cross-trained
on both fiber and RF.

## Responsibilities
- Operate boats safely to reach shoreline nodes and customer docks/premises.
- Build/maintain T2/T3 nodes: masts, enclosures, radios, PON, solar/battery, grounding.
- Install FTTH drops (fusion splicing, ONT) and wireless CPE (nLOS/PtMP); activate and
  speed-test; get customer sign-off.
- Diagnose and repair outages; carry and manage boat spare kits.
- Capture as-builts in the field PWA (GPS, serials, photos) — completeness required.
- Follow all safety and drinking-water-protection protocols.

## Requirements
- Comfort operating boats; willingness to work outdoors/on water year-round.
- Aptitude for fiber splicing and/or RF/radio installation (training provided).
- Basic networking (IP, PoE); careful, methodical, safety-first.
- Valid driver's license; ability to lift/climb per safety standards.

## Nice to have
- FOA CFOT; tower/climbing + fall-protection cert; boating safety cert; WISP/FTTH
  experience; Tarana/Cambium/Ubiquiti familiarity.

## Comp
Competitive hourly/base + on-call + marine premium; training and certs paid.
