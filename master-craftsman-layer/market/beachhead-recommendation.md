## The one dimension that can veto a metro: (b)

Dimension **b** is not just a score input — it is a gate. If a state's law does
**not** permit an unlicensed/augmented worker to perform trade work under a
remotely-supervising master's license, the entire operating model is illegal
there regardless of how attractive the labor and housing numbers look. This is
the same question as decision **D2** and must be answered by an attorney for the
finalist state, using the case pull scaffolded in `legal/supervision-risk-brief.md`,
before hiring. Do not let a high composite score paper over a red flag on (b).
