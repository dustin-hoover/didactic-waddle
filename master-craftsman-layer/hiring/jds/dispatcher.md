# Dispatcher

**Draft template — not posted.**

## The role
You are the air-traffic control for the fleet. You book jobs, route techs,
balance supervisor load, and keep customers informed — with software doing the
heavy lifting and you handling the judgment calls and the exceptions.

## What you'd do
- Own the day's board: assign techs + supervising masters, keep routes tight.
- Watch supervisor load so no master is booked past the lawful ratio.
- Handle the complexity the voice agent escalates — the anxious customer, the
  double-booking, the "we found something worse when we opened it up."
- Trigger the same-day scheduling that makes the "$79 tune-up, same-day" offer
  real.

## Testing for
- Calm under a full board. Organization. Warmth on the phone. Judgment about
  when a job needs a human decision, not a default.

## Pay
- Competitive local base + team bonus tied to on-time rate and customer NPS.

## Note
One of the three break-even roles (6 techs + 1 supervisor + 1 dispatcher).
