# Remote Supervising Master (HVAC)

**Draft template — not posted.**

## The role
You hold the master license. You supervise a small fleet of augmented field
technicians remotely — live audio/video from their headsets — diagnosing with
them, catching what they can't yet see, and signing off every job before it
closes. You are the safety layer and the quality bar.

## Who this is for
A master HVAC contractor (or equivalent) who's tired of the ladder and the
truck but not tired of the craft — someone who wants to multiply their judgment
across many jobs instead of doing one at a time.

## What you own
- Live supervision of 8–10 concurrent jobs (**final ratio set by the chosen
  state's law — see legal brief; this may be lower where the law requires**).
- Sign-off authority and responsibility. Your license, your standards.
- Mandatory synchronous live-view on every gas, main-panel, and roof-penetration
  task — non-negotiable, enforced by the software.

## Non-negotiables we'll hold you to
- No sign-off you didn't actually supervise. The console tracks live-view per
  sign-off; over-signing is the one thing that gets escalated to the founder.
- You flag risk early and loudly.

## Pay
- $65/hr (per finance model) + quarterly outcome bonuses (first-time-fix,
  callback, safety).

## Legal note (internal)
This role's legality *is* the company's legality. Do not extend an offer until
D2 (attorney greenlight of the remote-supervision model in the chosen state) is
cleared. The ratio in the posting must match what counsel confirms is lawful.
