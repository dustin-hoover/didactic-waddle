# Field Technician (Level 1)

**Draft template — not posted. No ad spend authorized (see EXECUTION_BOUNDARY).**

## The pitch
We pay you to learn a trade. No debt. Real career. You show up with grit and
good hands; we give you the training, the tools, the tech, and a master
craftsman in your ear on every job. Clear path to your own license and equity
for early hires.

## What you'd actually do
- Run residential HVAC service calls with an AR headset that walks you through
  each step and puts a licensed master on the line when you need one.
- Diagnose, fix, and confirm the fix — backed by real-time code lookup and a
  supervisor who signs off before the job closes.
- Treat every customer's home like your own.

## What we're testing for (not a résumé)
- **Pattern recognition** — can you tell what's different between two situations?
- **Mechanical reasoning** — do things make intuitive sense when you take them
  apart?
- **Customer empathy** — can you make a stressed homeowner feel taken care of?
- **Grit** — do you finish hard things?

No college required. No prior trade experience required. Must be 18+, able to
pass EPA Section 608 (we help you get it), and comfortable on a ladder/in an
attic.

## Pay & path
- $35/hr base (per finance model), quarterly bonuses tied to first-time-fix and
  callback rate.
- Registered with the state and working under a licensed master from day one.
- Structured path toward your own HVACR license; early hires get equity.

## Legal note (internal, not in the posting)
Title/scope of work must match the chosen state's registrant/apprentice rules
(see `legal/supervision-risk-brief.md`). Do not advertise a scope the state's
supervision law doesn't permit until counsel signs off (D2).
